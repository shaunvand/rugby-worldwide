Membership questions

What is the Global Rugby Community?

An international platform created by Rugby Management Worldwide that connects rugby players, coaches, clubs, schools, academies, teams, businesses and supporters from around the world. Members can access exclusive opportunities, benefits, networking and rugby-related services.

Who can join the Global Rugby Community?

Anyone involved in or passionate about rugby: players, coaches, parents, clubs, schools, academies, universities, rugby businesses and supporters from anywhere in the world.

What benefits do I receive as a member?

Benefits depend on your package and can include exclusive rugby opportunities, international networking, player and team opportunities, events, special offers, discounts and access to selected RMWW services.

Can I join if I live outside South Africa?

Absolutely. The community is designed to connect rugby people worldwide, and members from any country can join.

How do I choose the right membership package?

Compare the packages above and pick the one that suits your rugby goals, whether you are joining as an individual, player, coach, organisation or business. You can upgrade at any time.
