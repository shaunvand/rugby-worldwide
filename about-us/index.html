About us

About Rugby Management Worldwide

Founded by Warren Edwards, a former semi-pro player, to give young players the international tours and exposure he never had.

Who we are

Rugby Management Worldwide was founded by Warren Edwards, a former semi-pro player who spent years competing at a high level before turning his attention to the next generation of players.

Looking back on his own career, Warren often thought about the opportunities he never had: the international tours, the exposure to different styles of rugby, the chance to test himself against schools and academies from other countries while he was still young enough for it to shape him. Those experiences build players and people in ways a home season never can, and too many talented kids never get access to them.

That's the gap Rugby Management Worldwide was built to close. We give school teams, academies and individual players the chance to tour, compete and be seen on a bigger stage.

What we do

We organise international rugby tours and festival entries for school teams and academies, and we put together invitational squads for players who want in on the experience but don't have a team to travel with. Every trip is built around competitive rugby, genuine cultural exposure and the kind of camaraderie that turns teammates into friends for life.

Our approach

Having played the game at a high level, Warren brings a player's understanding of what a tour should deliver: proper competition, a well-run squad environment, and experiences on and off the field that are worth the trip.

Why choose us

Three reasons teams travel with us

  • Built by a player, for players

  • For teams and individuals
