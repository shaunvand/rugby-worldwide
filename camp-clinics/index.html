The venue

Delsma Farm: a secluded retreat in the Western Cape

Nestled in a picturesque landscape of mountains and farmland, 12 km from the village of Riebeek-Kasteel, Delsma Farm is an excellent backdrop for camps and team building. Players focus on refining skills, honing strategy and building team bonds.

Camp questions

What age is the Pre-Season Rugby Camp for?

Boys and girls aged 12 to 17.

What is included in the camp price?

Accommodation and meals for 5 days and 4 nights at Delsma Farm, plus the field and equipment.

What should my child bring?

Their own sleeping bag and towels, plus rugby kit.
