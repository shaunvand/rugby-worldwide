Meet the founder

Warren Edwards: built by a player, for players

Warren spent years playing semi-pro rugby at a high level. Looking back, he kept thinking about the chances he never had: international tours, different styles of rugby, and the chance to test himself against schools and academies from other countries while he was still young enough for it to shape him.

So he built Rugby Management Worldwide to close that gap, for school teams, academies and individual players who deserve a bigger stage.

Those experiences build players and people in ways a home season never can.

School tour questions

Which schools can tour with Rugby Management Worldwide?

School teams from South Africa or abroad. We also tour club teams and academies, and enter invitational squads for individual players.

What is included in a school rugby tour?

We plan it from start to finish: flights, accommodation and meals, transfers, match fixtures, training sessions, excursions, live pro matches and community service.

Where can a school rugby tour go?

South Africa and Africa, the UK and Ireland, Europe, the USA and Canada, South America, Australia and New Zealand, and China and Japan.

Can a school enter a sevens festival?

Yes. We enter school teams and academies into events such as the Dubai 7s and the Global Youth 7s.
