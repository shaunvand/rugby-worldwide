Common questions

Who can tour with Rugby Management Worldwide?

School teams, club teams, academies and pro teams, plus individual players: we enter an invitational team for players without a squad.

Where do your rugby tours go?

South Africa and Africa, the UK and Ireland, Europe, the USA and Canada, South America, Australia and New Zealand, and China and Japan.

What does a tour include?

We plan it from start to finish: flights, accommodation and meals, transfers, match fixtures, training sessions, excursions, live pro matches and community service.

When is the Global Elite 7s?

26 to 28 November 2027 in Cape Town, South Africa, for u23 men's and women's sevens teams.
